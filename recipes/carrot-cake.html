carrot cake recipe

Description

Ingredients

  • 275g (2 cups) self-raising flour
  • 175g (1 cup) caster sugar
  • 100g (1/2 cup) light brown sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 4 large eggs
  • 250ml (1 cup + 1 tbsp) sunflower or vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200g (2 cups) finely grated carrots

Steps

  1. Prep the Oven and Tins: Heat your oven to 180°C / 160°C fan / Gas 4. Grease and line the base and sides of two 20cm (8-inch) round cake tins with baking parchment.
  2. Mix Dry Ingredients: In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugars, cinnamon, nutmeg, bicarbonate of soda, and salt.
  3. Mix Wet Ingredients: In a separate jug or bowl, whisk together the eggs, vegetable oil, and vanilla extract.
  4. Combine: Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients. Stir gently with a spoon or spatula until just combined.
  5. Add Carrots and Extras: Fold in the grated carrots, and the walnuts and raisins if you are using them.
  6. Bake: Divide the batter evenly between your two prepared tins. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, or until a skewer inserted into the center comes out clean. Let the cakes cool completely on a wire rack.
  7. Make the Frosting: Beat the softened butter until smooth, then add the cream cheese and vanilla extract. Gradually sift in the icing sugar, beating continuously until light and fluffy.
  8. Assemble: Spread a layer of frosting on top of one cooled cake layer, place the second layer on top, and cover the top and sides with the remaining frosting. Decorate with extra chopped nuts if desired.
