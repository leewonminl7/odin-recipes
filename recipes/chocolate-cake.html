chocolate cake recipe

Description

Ingredients

  • 200g golden caster sugar
  • 200g softened unsalted butter
  • 4 large eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp milk

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 190°C / 170°C fan / gas 5. Grease and line two 20cm round cake tins.
  2. Mix the batter: Combine all the cake ingredients into a large bowl and beat until smooth and creamy.
  3. Bake: Divide the batter evenly between the two tins. Bake in the oven for 20 minutes until risen and springy to the touch.
  4. Cool: Leave the cakes to cool completely on a wire rack.
  5. Make the buttercream: Melt the chopped milk chocolate and let it cool slightly. Beat the softened butter until smooth, then mix in the icing sugar, cocoa powder, milk, and melted chocolate until fluffy.
  6. Assemble: Sandwich the two cooled sponges together with a layer of buttercream, then use the rest to cover the top and sides.
